Flash Player is missing

Dungeon Blitz R runs in Adobe Flash Player, which normally comes with the launcher. It isn’t in the launcher’s folder, and the launcher didn’t find another copy it can use on this computer.

Reinstalling the launcher puts it back. Or choose a pepflashplayer64.dll yourself: it has to be the 64-bit Pepper build, version 32.0.0.363 or older (later versions stop working after January 2021).
